AR LAB

Holographic models you grab out of the air, turn, scale and pull apart with your hands.

This is a separate app from the powers sim on purpose. That one runs three tracking models at once (hands, face, body), which eats frame time and makes the hand gestures less certain. This one loads only the hand tracker, so grabs are faster and steadier. Everything below picks what gets loaded before anything starts.
